## 摘要
敬畏是一种积极情绪，常伴随着浩瀚与合一感，对幸福感和社交行为有已知的益处。然而，其神经基础仍知之甚少。我们记录了23位健康老年参与者的脑电图和自主神经生理活动，同时他们观看了一段基于自然的视听影片并主观评价了敬畏事件。敬畏是报告的主要情绪——尽管其他积极情绪（如喜悦）也被高度评价。敬畏事件与皮肤电导水平下降，以及脑电图α和θ频谱功率降低相关——这些生理变化与低唤醒和正向效价的情绪状态相关。有趣的是，敬畏事件表现出Lempel-Ziv复杂度增加——表明神经信号熵增强和意识体验的丰富性增加。LZC还与敬畏评分强度正相关，与皮肤电导水平负相关。三个使用独立诱导方法（视频片段和通过N,N-二甲基色胺的药理诱导）的额外数据集也显示，枕叶LZC与敬畏正相关——表明LZC作为敬畏的神经生理标志物具有一定的普适性。这些结果表明，敬畏诱发了与主观情感体验相关的神经生理状态。

## Abstract
Awe is a positive emotion often accompanied by sensations of vastness and unity, with known benefits for well-being and social behavior. However, its neural underpinnings remain poorly understood. We recorded electroencephalography (EEG) and autonomic physiology in 23 healthy older participants while they watched a nature-based audiovisual film and subjectively rated awe events. Awe was the predominant emotion reported - though other positive emotions (e.g., joy) were also highly rated. Awe events were associated with decreased skin conductance level (SCL), and decreased EEG alpha and theta spectral power - physiological changes associated with low arousal and positively valenced emotional states. Interestingly, awe events exhibited increased Lempel Ziv Complexity (LZC) - indicating heightened neural signal entropy and increased richness of the conscious experience. LZC was also positively associated with the intensity of the awe ratings and negatively associated with SCL. Three additional datasets with separate independent induction methods (video clips and pharmacological induction via N,N-dimethyltryptamine) also showed positive occipital LZC associations with awe - suggesting some generalizability of LZC as a neurophysiological marker of awe. These results suggest that awe evokes neurophysiological states linked to the subjective affective experience.
