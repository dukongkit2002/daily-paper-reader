## 摘要
我们研究了一项两步回溯线索任务中优先化的效应，在该任务中，男女受试者在工作记忆中保持两个项目，且未被第一个线索提示的项目不能被丢弃，因为它可能被第二个线索优先化。在实验1中，采用密集采样程序，优先化任务中的回忆表现以15 Hz振荡，而匹配的中性线索任务中以20 Hz振荡。此外，优先化项目受到保护，免受未提示项目以及前一试次项目产生的偏差影响。在实验2中，我们在受试者执行两项任务的变体时寻找这些效应的脑电图相关性。优先化线索独特地触发了15 Hz的相位重置和该频率下振荡峰值的增加。突发分析排除了突发作为可能的潜在因素。时间分辨表征相似性分析（RSA）表明，优先化线索触发的表征转换对于未提示项目更大。这些结果提供了证据，表明优先化的屏蔽效应可能源于未优先化项目转换到未优先化状态，这种状态由一个以15 Hz循环的机制实现和维持。

## Abstract
We studied the effects of prioritization in a two-step retrocuing task in which male and female humans hold two items in working memory, and the item not cued by the first cue cannot be dropped because it may be prioritized by the second cue. In Experiment 1, using a dense sampling procedure, recall performance oscillated at 15 Hz in the prioritization task, in comparison to 20 Hz in a matched neutral-cue task. Furthermore, the prioritized item was shielded from bias exerted by the uncued item, as well as by items from the previous trial. In Experiment 2, we sought EEG correlates of these effects while participants performed variants of the two tasks. The prioritization cue uniquely triggered a phase reset at 15 Hz and an increase in oscillatory peaks at this frequency. Burst analysis ruled out bursting as a possible underlying factor. Time-resolved representational similarity analysis (RSA) revealed that the prioritization cue triggered representational transformations that were larger for the uncued item. These results provide evidence that the shielding effects of prioritization may arise from the transformation of the not-prioritized item into an unprioritized state that is implemented and maintained by a mechanism that cycles at 15 Hz.
