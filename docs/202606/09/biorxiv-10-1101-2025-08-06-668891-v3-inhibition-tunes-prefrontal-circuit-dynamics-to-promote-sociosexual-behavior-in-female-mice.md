## 摘要
前额叶皮层（PFC）在多种社会行为的选择和表达中起着核心作用。兴奋与抑制的平衡对正常的社会功能是必要的，但抑制如何塑造锥体细胞活动以促进特定社会行为仍不清楚。为了解决这个问题，我们开发了一项新颖的决策任务，其中雌性小鼠在与雄性交互（社会性刺激）或与一种有食欲的非社会选项（牛奶溶液）之间进行选择。为了探索任务相关的抑制性亚群的作用，我们靶向了内侧前额叶皮层（mPFC）中表达催产素受体的神经元（OXTR神经元）。结合对OXTR神经元的光遗传学抑制和锥体细胞神经活动的群体钙成像，我们发现OXTR神经元通常会促进与雄性的交互，而非非社会替代选项。OXTR神经元还调节锥体细胞活动，从而使特定的神经元集群在决策过程中代表雄性选项。计算模型通过一种锥体细胞竞争机制再现了这些发现，在该机制中，OXTR神经元的调节使得代表雄性的锥体细胞集群能够有效地与剩余的锥体细胞群体竞争，从而驱动对雄性的选择。这些结果提供了一种候选机制，即抑制使得mPFC锥体细胞活动能够选择特定的社会行为，并可能有助于解释为何兴奋/抑制平衡对社会功能如此重要。

## Abstract
The prefrontal cortex (PFC) plays a central role in the selection and expression of diverse social behaviors. A balance of excitation and inhibition is necessary for normal social functioning, but it remains unclear how inhibition sculpts pyramidal activity to promote specific social behaviors. To address this question, we developed a novel decision-making task in which female mice chose between interaction with a male (sociosexual stimulus) or an appetitive non-social option (milk solution). To explore the role of a task-relevant inhibitory subpopulation, we targeted neurons in the medial PFC (mPFC) expressing oxytocin receptors (OXTR neurons). Combining optogenetic inhibition of OXTR neurons with population calcium imaging of pyramidal neural activity, we found that OXTR neurons normally promote interaction with a male compared to a non-social alternative. OXTR neurons also regulate pyramidal activity, which enables a specific ensemble to represent the male option during decision-making. Computational modeling reproduced these findings through a pyramidal competition mechanism, in which regulation by OXTR neurons allows a male-representing pyramidal ensemble to effectively compete against the remaining pyramidal population to drive male choice. These results provide a candidate mechanism by which inhibition enables mPFC pyramidal activity to select for specific social behaviors and may help explain why excitation/inhibition balance is so important for social functioning.
