## 摘要
基底前脑胆碱能神经元调节皮层活动与认知，并在阿尔茨海默病（AD）中易受损伤。然而，控制特定投射的基底前脑胆碱能神经元群体的上游环路仍未完全阐明。本研究利用投射特异性狂犬病病毒介导的单突触逆行示踪技术，绘制了投射至内侧前额叶皮层（mPFC）的迈内特基底核（NBM）和布罗卡斜角带水平肢（HDB）胆碱能神经元的全脑输入图谱。投射至mPFC的NBM与HDB胆碱能神经元接受广泛但存在差异的输入模式。NBM胆碱能神经元接受显著的纹状体输入，包括来自表达D1受体的中等多棘神经元的输入，而HDB胆碱能神经元则显示相对较弱的纹状体输入及更广泛的非纹状体来源。光遗传学电生理实验证实，纹状体输入与NBM胆碱能神经元形成单突触GABA能抑制性突触联系。这种抑制性传递在5xFAD小鼠中减弱，表明在AD小鼠模型中纹状体-NBM抑制性环路受损。综上所述，这些发现揭示了投射至mPFC的基底前脑胆碱能神经元的亚区特异性输入组织，并确定了一条在AD中易损的纹状体-NBM环路。

亮点：全脑狂犬病病毒示踪揭示mPFC投射基底前脑胆碱能神经元的输入组织。投射至mPFC的NBM与HDB胆碱能神经元表现出不同的单突触输入特征。纹状体D1-MSN是mPFC投射NBM胆碱能神经元的主要输入来源。纹状体-NBM抑制性传递在5xFAD小鼠中功能受损。

## Abstract
Basal forebrain cholinergic neurons regulate cortical activity and cognition and are vulnerable in Alzheimers disease (AD). However, the upstream circuits controlling projection-defined basal forebrain cholinergic populations remain incompletely understood. Here, we used projection-specific rabies-mediated monosynaptic tracing to map whole-brain inputs to medial prefrontal cortex (mPFC)-projecting cholinergic neurons in the nucleus basalis of Meynert (NBM) and horizontal limb of the diagonal band of Broca (HDB). mPFC-projecting NBM and HDB cholinergic neurons received broad but distinct input patterns. NBM cholinergic neurons received prominent striatal input, including input from D1-expressing medium spiny neurons, whereas HDB cholinergic neurons showed proportionally weaker striatal input and broader non-striatal contributions. Optogenetic electrophysiology confirmed that striatal inputs formed monosynaptic GABAergic inhibitory synapses onto NBM cholinergic neurons. This inhibitory transmission was weakened in 5xFAD mice, indicating impairment of a striatal-NBM inhibitory circuit in an AD mouse model. Together, these findings reveal subregion-specific input organization of mPFC-projecting basal forebrain cholinergic neurons and identify a vulnerable striatal-NBM circuit in AD.
