## 摘要
多元时间序列的网络分析在神经科学到地震学等许多领域中都很流行。逆谱密度是时间序列网络分析的常用选择，因为它表示在去除所有其他变量的最佳线性预测后两个变量之间的频域相关性。在许多应用中，目标是研究这些网络在不同条件下如何变化。例如，在神经科学中，人们可能关注刺激前后大脑连接网络如何变化。为了实现这一目标，我们基于对两个高维逆谱密度之差的直接估计，开发了一个推断框架。我们为任何去偏 D-trace 估计程序开发了一个新的高斯近似误差界，然后利用该误差界来确定谱密度 Welch 估计器的最优窗宽，并建立我们的去偏 D-trace 估计器的渐近正态性。此外，我们基于广义 D-trace 估计程序开发了一种高效算法，以克服高维推断的计算复杂性。该方法在合成数据实验和脑电图数据实验中进行了验证。

## Abstract
Network analysis for multivariate time series is popular in many fields, from neuroscience to seismology. The inverse spectral density is a common choice for time series network analysis due to its representation of the frequency domain correlation between two variables after removing the best linear predictor of all other variables. In many applications, the goal is to study how these networks change across different conditions. For example, in neuroscience, one might be interested in how the brain connectivity network changes before and after stimulation. Towards this goal, we develop an inference framework based on a direct estimate of the difference in two high-dimensional inverse spectral densities. We develop a new Gaussian approximation error bound for any de-biased D-trace estimation procedure which is then leveraged to both inform optimal window sizes of Welch's estimators of the spectral density and establish asymptotic normality of our de-biased D-trace estimator. Moreover, we develop an efficient algorithm based on a generalized D-trace estimation procedure to overcome the computational complexity of high-dimensional inference. The method is illustrated on synthetic data experiments and on experiments with electroencephalography data.
