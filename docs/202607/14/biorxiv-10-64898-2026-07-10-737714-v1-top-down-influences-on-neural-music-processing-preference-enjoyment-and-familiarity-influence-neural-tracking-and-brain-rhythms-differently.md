## 摘要
音乐享受和熟悉度密切相关，但在神经音乐加工研究中常被混淆。本研究利用脑电图（EEG）考察了它们对皮层振荡活动和音乐神经追踪的独特贡献。32名参与者聆听了自选的最喜爱歌曲、近期喜爱歌曲以及来自不喜爱音乐类型的速度匹配歌曲。这种新颖的实验范式通过包含高度享受但熟悉度不同的歌曲，将熟悉度与享受度分离。使用线性混合效应模型，以享受度和熟悉度评分分析频谱功率和皮层追踪（使用互信息）。熟悉度与左额叶α功率的增加相关，而享受度预测了θ和β功率的增加，表明了这些维度各自独特的振荡特征。交互作用显示，享受度与θ功率的正相关关系在高度熟悉的音乐中最强。皮层追踪分析表明，更高的享受度与δ频段追踪的减少相关，且存在显著交互作用，表明这一负相关关系存在于高度熟悉的歌曲中，但在熟悉度较低的歌曲中不成立。这些发现表明，享受度和熟悉度以不同方式塑造了对音乐的神经反应，并强调了建模这两个因素以区分它们在音乐聆听过程中对神经活动的独特影响的重要性。

## Abstract
Music enjoyment and familiarity are closely related but often confounded in studies of neural music processing. Here, we investigated their distinct contributions to cortical oscillatory activity and neural tracking of music using electroencephalography (EEG). Thirty-two participants listened to self-selected all-time favourite songs, recent favourite songs, and tempo-matched songs from disliked genres. This novel paradigm dissociated familiarity from enjoyment by including highly enjoyed songs that differed in familiarity. Spectral power and cortical tracking (using Mutual Information) were analysed using linear mixed-effects models with enjoyment and familiarity ratings. Familiarity was associated with increased left-frontal alpha power, whereas enjoyment predicted increased theta and beta power, demonstrating distinct oscillatory signatures for these dimensions. An interaction revealed that the positive relationship between enjoyment and theta power was strongest for highly familiar music. Cortical tracking analyses showed that greater enjoyment was associated with reduced delta-band tracking, with a significant interaction indicating that this negative relationship was present for highly familiar songs but not for less familiar songs. These findings indicate that enjoyment and familiarity differentially shape neural responses to music and highlight the importance of modelling both factors to disentangle their distinct effects on neural activity during music listening.

- **享受度和熟悉度是行为上密切关联的**：歌曲越熟悉，参与者对其的享受度越高，印证了“纯粹曝光效应”。

    - **解释**：对高度熟悉且享受的音乐，大脑可能更少依赖声音的初级声学特征（δ追踪），而转向更高层级的预测和加工；对于不熟悉但享受的音乐，注意力驱动的追踪可能增强。

- **新颖的实验范式**：成功地将“享受度”和“熟悉度”这两个高度相关的变量进行了解耦，是该领域的一个关键方法学贡献。
