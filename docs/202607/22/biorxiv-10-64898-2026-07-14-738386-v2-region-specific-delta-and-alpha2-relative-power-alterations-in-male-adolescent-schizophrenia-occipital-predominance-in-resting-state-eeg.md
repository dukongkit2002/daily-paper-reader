## 摘要
静息态脑电图（EEG）研究一致报告精神分裂症中慢波活动增加和α功率降低。然而，EEG相对功率（RP）在更精细频段上的区域分布，特别是在青少年精神分裂症中，仍未被充分描述。我们分析了一个公开可用的静息态EEG数据集，包含45名男性青少年精神分裂症患者（SCZ；平均年龄：12.3±1.2岁）和39名年龄和性别匹配的健康对照（CON；平均年龄：12.3±1.3岁），采用16个头皮电极（10-20系统）记录。经过带通滤波、伪影子空间重建、样条插值、平均重参考和独立成分分析后，计算了五个皮层区域（额叶、中央、顶叶、颞叶和枕叶）九个频段的RP。组间差异采用三因素重复测量方差分析（ANOVA）评估，随后使用估计边际均值（emmeans）进行Bonferroni错误发现率（FDR）校正的事后成对比较。与对照组相比，SCZ组在额叶、顶叶和枕叶区域显示出显著较高的δ RP，在中央、顶叶、颞叶和枕叶区域显示出显著较低的α2 RP（与α1可分离）（所有FDR < 0.001）。枕叶皮层表现出最大的δ RP增加（|Cohen's d|=0.82）和最大的α2 RP降低（|d|=0.73），效应量为中到大。地形图显示SCZ组广泛的δ增强和枕顶α2活动减弱。这些发现揭示了青少年精神分裂症静息态EEG相对功率的脑区特异性改变，特别是在枕叶皮层内，并表明区域RP可能作为早发性精神分裂症的一个有前景的神经生理特征。

## Abstract
Resting-state electroencephalography (EEG) studies consistently report increased slow-wave activity and reduced alpha power in schizophrenia. However, the regional distribution of EEG relative power (RP) across finer frequency bands, particularly in adolescent schizophrenia, remains poorly characterized. We analyzed a publicly available resting-state EEG dataset comprising 45 male adolescents with schizophrenia (SCZ; mean age: 12.3 {+/-} 1.2 years) and 39 age- and sex-matched healthy controls (CON; mean age: 12.3 {+/-} 1.3years), recorded using 16 scalp electrodes (10-20 system). Following band-pass filtering, artefact subspace reconstruction, spline interpolation, average re-referencing and independent component analysis, RP was computed for nine frequency bands across five cortical regions (frontal, central, parietal, temporal, and occipital). Group differences were assessed using three-way repeated-measures analysis of variance (ANOVA), followed by Bonferroni false discovery rate (FDR)-corrected post-hoc pairwise comparisons using estimated marginal means (emmeans). Compared to controls, the SCZ group showed significantly higher delta RP in the frontal, parietal, and occipital regions and significantly lower alpha2 RP, dissociable from alpha1, in the central, parietal, temporal, and occipital regions (all FDR < 0.001). The occipital cortex showed the greatest increase in delta RP (|Cohens d|=0.82) and the greatest reduction in alpha2 RP (|d|=0.73), with moderate-to-large effect sizes. Topographic maps demonstrated widespread delta enhancement and attenuated occipito-parietal alpha2 activity in the SCZ group. These findings demonstrate region-specific alterations in resting-state EEG relative power in adolescent schizophrenia, particularly within the occipital cortex, and suggest that regional RP may serve as a promising neurophysiological feature for early-onset schizophrenia.
