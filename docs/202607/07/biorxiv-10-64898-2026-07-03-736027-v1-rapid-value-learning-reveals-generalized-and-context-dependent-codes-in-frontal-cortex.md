## 摘要
研究大脑如何表征价值涉及不同的方法和训练历史，从任务初始状态人类的神经成像到经过广泛训练的非人类灵长类动物的单神经元记录。跨领域的相似发现鼓励了一个未经检验的假设，即迅速出现的和过度训练的价值表征是等价的。我们记录了猕猴前扣带皮层（ACC）和眶额皮层（OFC）的单神经元活动，当它们学习新的线索价值并在新的和过度训练的线索之间进行选择时。价值反应在4-7次线索呈现内出现，与行为适应相匹配。然而ACC和OFC使用了不同的编码。ACC以一种通用的格式编码价值，这种格式泛化到训练历史之外。OFC编码更具情境依赖性，由选择经验招募了不同的亚群。选择新颖性也在选择价值信号出现之前独立于价值被编码。在学习过程中，ACC反应从过度训练的次级强化物转移到新预测性线索。这些发现建立了比较跨方法、物种和训练历史的价值表征所需的条件（快速获取）和足够条件（泛化格式）。

## Abstract
Studying how the brain represents value spans distinct methods and training histories, from neuroimaging in task-naive humans to single-neuron recordings in extensively trained non-human primates. Similar findings across fields have encouraged the untested assumption that rapidly emerging and overtrained value representations are equivalent. Here we recorded single-neuron activity in anterior cingulate cortex (ACC) and orbitofrontal cortex (OFC) as macaques learned novel cue values and chose between novel and overtrained cues. Value responses emerged within 4-7 cue presentations, matching behavioural adaptation. Yet ACC and OFC used distinct codes. ACC encoded value in a common format that generalised across training history. OFC coding was more context-dependent, with distinct subpopulations recruited by choice experience. Choice novelty was also encoded independently of value before chosen value signals emerged. During learning, ACC responses shifted from overtrained secondary reinforcers to newly predictive cues. These findings establish the necessary (rapid acquisition) and sufficient (generalised format) conditions for comparing value representations across methods, species, and training histories.
