## 摘要
背景 由于认为大麻二酚（CBD）相对于Δ9-四氢大麻酚（THC）更安全，孕期使用CBD的情况日益增多。然而，妊娠期CBD暴露的神经发育风险在很大程度上仍未明确。

方法 采用性别分层的框架，我们研究了在子宫内暴露于THC或CBD（妊娠第5-18天，3 mg/kg）的成年（P100-140）小鼠后代。通过风险评估和重复行为任务评估其行为策略，并结合对内侧前额叶皮层第5层神经元（负责趋避决策的核心枢纽）的靶向电生理学图谱分析。

结果 我们发现，重复行为的增加是产前大麻素暴露的普遍特征。风险评估的改变仅在CBD暴露的雌性中出现，且看似与经典的焦虑指标无关。在回路层面，THC和CBD均与内源性大麻素介导的长时程抑制（eCB-LTD）缺失相关。在雄性中，CBD暴露同时伴随着双向可塑性的崩溃，表现为功能饱和、AMPA/NMDA比值升高以及NMDAR激活动力学减慢。这种天花板效应可能代表了前额叶输出回路的自上而下约束，潜在地限制了通常与适应性行为转换相关的突触灵活性。相比之下，雌性表现出化合物特异性的兴奋/抑制平衡重组。CBD暴露的雌性呈现出一种按比例放大的架构，维持了净兴奋/抑制平衡，而THC则通过抑制性控制的崩溃与促兴奋表型相关。

结论 尽管eCB-LTD的丧失是共同特征，但不同的突触重塑可能构成了风险评估和行为灵活性差异改变的基础。这种性别特异性的回路重塑为妊娠期大麻素暴露相关的长期行为风险提供了神经生物学框架。

## Abstract
BackgroundPrenatal cannabidiol (CBD) consumption is increasing, driven by a perception of safety relative to {Delta}9-tetrahydrocannabinol (THC). However, the neurodevelopmental risks of gestational CBD remain largely uncharacterized.

MethodsUsing a sex-disaggregated framework, we investigated adult (P100-140) mouse offspring following in utero exposure (GD5-18; 3 mg/kg) to THC or CBD. Behavioral strategies were evaluated through risk-assessment and repetitive behavior tasks, coupled with targeted electrophysiological mapping of medial prefrontal cortex Layer 5 neurons, the primary hub for approach-avoidance arbitration.

ResultsWe found that increased repetitive behavior was a universal feature of prenatal cannabinoids exposure. Alterations in risk appraisal emerged uniquely in CBD-exposed females and appeared dissociated from classical anxiety metrics. At the circuit level, THC and CBD were linked to an absence of endocannabinoid long-term depression (eCB-LTD). In males, CBD exposure coincided with a bidirectional plasticity collapse characterized by functional saturation, elevated AMPA/NMDA ratios, and slowed NMDAR activation kinetics. This ceiling effect may represent a top-down constraint on the prefrontal output circuit, potentially limiting the synaptic flexibility typically associated with adaptive behavioral transitions. In contrast, females exhibited compound-specific reorganizations of E/I balance. CBD-exposed females displayed a scaled-up architecture that preserved net E/I balance, whereas THC was associated with a pro-excitatory phenotype through the collapse of inhibitory control.

ConclusionsDespite a shared loss of eCB-LTD, distinct synaptic remodeling might underlie divergent alterations in risk assessment and behavioral flexibility. This sex-specific circuit rewiring provides a neurobiological framework for the long-term behavioral risks associated with gestational cannabinoid exposure.
