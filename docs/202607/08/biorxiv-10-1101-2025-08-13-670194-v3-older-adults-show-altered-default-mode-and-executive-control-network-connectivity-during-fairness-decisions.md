## 摘要
公平相关决策依赖于出价价值和伙伴身份，但衰老如何重塑在不同社会情境下支持这些决策的网络水平连接仍不清楚。较年轻（18-35岁）和较年长（65-80岁）的成年人在接受功能性磁共振成像扫描的同时，对最后通牒博弈中来自年龄相似或相异的虚拟伙伴的出价做出反应。我们根据出价大小、年龄组和伙伴相似性对逐次试验的选择进行建模，并检查了全脑激活以及默认模式网络（DMN）和执行控制网络（ECN）的任务依赖性连接。参与者更频繁地接受更公平的出价，但行为敏感性在年龄组或伙伴相似性方面没有可靠差异。全脑激活分析未显示显著的年龄与伙伴相似性交互效应。然而，连接分析揭示了相反的年龄相关模式。较年轻成人对相似伙伴表现出更强的受公平调节的DMN-前扣带回连接，而较年长成人则表现出相反的模式。年龄还调节了个体对公平规范违反的敏感性与ECN-内侧前额叶连接之间的关系：这一关联在较年轻成人中为负，在较年长成人中为正。这些发现表明，年轻和年长成人的公平决策可能由不同的大尺度网络连接模式支持。

## Abstract
Fairness-related decisions depend on both offer value and partner identity, yet how aging reshapes the network-level connectivity supporting these decisions across social contexts remains unknown. Younger (18-35) and older (65-80) adults underwent fMRI while responding to offers in an ultimatum game from ostensible partners similar or dissimilar in age. We modeled trial-by-trial choices by offer size, age group, and partner similarity, and examined whole-brain activation and task-dependent connectivity of the default mode (DMN) and executive control (ECN) networks. Participants accepted fairer offers more often, but behavioral sensitivity did not differ reliably by age group or partner similarity. Whole-brain activation analyses revealed no significant age-by-partner-similarity effects. Connectivity analyses, however, revealed opposing age-related patterns. Younger adults showed stronger fairness-modulated DMN-anterior cingulate connectivity for similar than for dissimilar partners, whereas older adults showed the reverse. Age also moderated the relationship between individual sensitivity to fairness norm violations and ECN-medial prefrontal connectivity: this association was negative in younger adults and positive in older adults. These findings suggest that decisions regarding fairness in younger and older adults may be supported by different patterns of large-scale network connectivity.
