## 摘要
静息态脑电图（rsEEG）提供了稳健的衰老指标，尤其是个体α峰值频率（iAPF）、α功率和非周期指数。这些指标究竟反映稳定特质还是会随认知努力而改变，仍未有定论，这对生命全程研究和临床研究具有直接影响。我们在一项成人寿命队列（N=390，年龄20-70岁）、一项五年纵向随访（N=100）以及一个完成不同任务的独立老年人数据集（N=71）中，对认知任务前后的周期性和非周期性rsEEG活动进行了参数化。在所有数据集中，认知努力导致整个寿命期内iAPF减慢及相关功率增加。值得注意的是，非周期性变化具有年龄依赖性，年轻成人任务后指数变陡，并随年龄增长逐渐变平，从而导致年龄对任务后指数的影响更强。我们的结果表明，广泛使用的频谱指标表现出急性状态依赖性，任务后记录为衡量健康衰老和病理中的个体差异提供了一个有前景的转化框架。

## Abstract
Resting-state electroencephalography (rsEEG) yields robust indices of ageing, notably individual alpha peak frequency (iAPF), alpha power, and the aperiodic exponent. Whether these markers reflect stable traits or shift with cognitive exertion remains unresolved, with direct consequences for lifespan and clinical research. We parameterised periodic and aperiodic rsEEG activity before and after cognitive tasks in a lifespan cohort (N = 390, aged 20-70), a five-year longitudinal follow-up (N = 100), and an independent older-adult dataset completing a different task (N = 71). Across datasets, cognitive exertion produced lifespan-wide iAPF slowing and increase in associated power. Notably, aperiodic shifts were age-dependent, with post-task steepening of the exponent in younger adults that progressively flattened with advancing age, resulting in a stronger effect of age on post-task exponents. Our results demonstrate that widely used spectral metrics exhibit acute state-dependency and post-task recordings offer a promising translational framework for indexing individual differences in healthy ageing and pathology.
